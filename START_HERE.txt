## 📊 Key Takeaways

### The Principle
> Fine-tune for stable knowledge.  
> Use RAG for volatile knowledge.

### The Cost Winner
For 1M queries/month:
- Cloud LLM + RAG: $5,800/month
- Fine-tuning only: $1,500/month
- **Hybrid approach: $1,300/month** ← Winner

### The Model Choices
- **Speed & Production**: Phi-3 Mini (3.8B)
- **Accuracy**: Mistral 7B (7B)
- **Edge/Offline**: TinyLlama (1.1B)
- **Long Docs**: Qwen (3B, 262K context)
